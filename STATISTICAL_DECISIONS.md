# Statistical decisions for the 12-run article-two study

The dataset has 3 seed blocks × 2 synapsin concentrations × 2 radius arms.
Each block contains four completed trajectories. Saved frames are correlated
within a run; the two radius arms share a seed label and a factorial design.
This file records which suggested follow-ups were adopted without presenting
analytical choices as additional independent experimental evidence.

| Suggestion | Decision and evidence |
|---|---|
| 10,000 classifier permutations | Replaced by **all eight** joint concentration-label swaps across the three seed blocks, preserving both radius arms in each block and refitting the full leave-one-seed-out pipeline. Observed balanced accuracy 1.0; 2/8 remappings also yield 1.0. A Monte Carlo sample of 10,000 draws from eight distinct admissible remappings adds no resolution. Because conditions were imposed, call 0.25 a descriptive remapping tail fraction, not a calibrated population/causal `p`. |
| Pool 3+3 signs to claim `p=0.03125` | **Rejected.** Divergent RNG consumption does not establish independence across radius arms sharing a seed. All six arm-specific concentration contrasts are positive, but the blockwise average has only three values and an exact two-sided sign-flip value of 0.25. |
| Explain exact-test floor | **Adopted.** With three paired nonzero block contrasts, a two-sided sign-flip test has a minimum tail probability of 2/8=0.25. The often quoted n=5 floor is 0.0625, n=6 floor 0.03125, but this design does not have six independent blocks. |
| Put inference frame before Results | **Adopted** in the manuscript. Magnitudes/intervals are reported without threshold-based selection. |
| Huge paired standardized effects | Calculated and available in `outputs/paired_contrasts.csv`: `d_z` for largest-component vesicle fraction is about **243** (mono) and **55** (poly). Not used as rhetorical evidence: n=3 makes SD unstable, the response is bounded, the high group is near a ceiling, and standardized effects are not biologically portable across different simulation protocols. Raw paired differences and intervals remain primary. |
| Bayesian BEST with three pairs | **Not adopted** for the paper. A posterior tail would be sensitive to an unvalidated prior and likelihood at n=3; it would not increase the amount of independent evidence. A prospectively justified prior could make this a future sensitivity analysis. |
| NPC across correlated endpoints | **Not adopted** as a separate significance claim. Joint blockwise remapping preserves endpoint dependence, but the three-block exact-test resolution remains eight patterns; combining nine graph-derived features does not manufacture independent replications. Multivariate results remain descriptive. |
| Integrated autocorrelation time on 60 late frames | **Not adopted** as an effective-sample-size assertion. Several endpoints are near a ceiling or still drift within the late window, making a stationary IAT estimate unstable. The analysis never counts frames as independent replicates. |
| Early-window comparison | **Adopted.** At the first saved frame (0.14 ms), condition means of largest-component SV fraction are 0.13 (10/mono), 0.10 (10/poly), 0.20 (60/mono), 0.17 (60/poly). They are *not* identical at first observation, so the data cannot rule out initialization/very-early numerical contributions. |
| TOST-style radius-equivalence margin | **Not adopted** as a formal equivalence test. A proposed ±0.05 margin was chosen after viewing the data and has no independent biological justification. The two size-contrast 95% intervals for largest-component SV fraction happen to fit inside it, but this is only a descriptive compatibility statement. |
| Many more ideal-gas null replicates | **Deferred.** The four-replicate geometric null is a supplementary RDF descriptor, not a primary inferential comparison. Increasing its replicas would require regenerating the RDF reference and checking the effect on eligible bins. No physical simulation is needed, but claiming an empirical `p` from this null would still be wrong: it corrects domain geometry only, not interactions or excluded volume. |

Reproduce the adopted checks with `python -m analysis.inference_checks` after
`python -m analysis.run`. The detailed eight accuracies, seed-level paired
differences, and early-window summaries are written to
`outputs/inference_checks.json`. Publication figures are generated by
`python -m analysis.paper_figures`. Neither script changes the archived
HDF5 trajectories.
